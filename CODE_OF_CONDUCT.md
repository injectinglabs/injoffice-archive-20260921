# Community code of conduct

We want participation in InjOffice to be constructive, safe, and welcoming.

Contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, accept good-faith technical disagreement, give actionable feedback, and avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, or publishing another person's private information. These expectations apply in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers, community spaces, and public interactions made on behalf of the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's verified contact on the GitHub organization profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. A maintainer involved in a report must not be the sole person deciding its outcome.
