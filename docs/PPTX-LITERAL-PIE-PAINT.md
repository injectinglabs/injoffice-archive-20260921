# Literal pie source paint

The opt-in literal pie preview accepts complete local series paint and sparse, explicitly indexed point overrides. For example, an explicit blue series with a red `dPt` at index 1 produces blue/red/blue slices for three literal values. Point override XML order does not change value order. The native record contains the resolved color at each value index; `createNativeLiteralPiePaths` and the playground's literal chart checkbox use those colors without another style resolver.

Qualification requires `varyColors=0`, a series `spPr` consisting of an explicit solid sRGB fill and explicit no-line, and/or complete point `spPr` records of that same form. A complete point paint overrides series paint at its `idx`. Without series paint every point must have complete point paint. Duplicate, out-of-range or noncanonical indices, empty or partial styles, theme colors, gradients, effects and painted lines refuse the preview. No palette or missing paint is guessed. Series paint precedes point records, as required by the chart series content model.

This extends source qualification only. Charts remain preserve-only; the source package is not rewritten. The existing `literal-pie-v1` profile, positive integer literal values, 64-point budget and approximate polygon geometry remain unchanged. Formula caches, legends, labels, 3D, explosion and unqualified defaults remain outside the profile. Qualification demonstrates source-derived preview behavior, not PowerPoint rendering parity.

Source basis: ECMA-376 Part 1, sections 21.2.2.52 (`dPt`), 21.2.2.84 (`idx`), and 21.2.2.197 (`spPr`). The point index identifies its data point; local shape properties describe their parent element's formatting. Applying complete series paint to unoverridden points and complete point paint to its indexed point is the narrow local precedence implemented here, without claiming a general chart style cascade. See the primary Open XML documentation for [pie series](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.drawing.charts.piechartseries?view=openxml-3.0.1), [data points](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.drawing.charts.datapoint?view=openxml-3.0.1), and [chart shape properties](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/documentformat.openxml.drawing.charts.chartshapeproperties?view=openxml-3.0.1).
